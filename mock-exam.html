Part A · Measurement, Precision & Scientific Notation

20 points

4 pts1

A technician records 0.0030400 kg. How many significant figures are present? Explain which zeros count.

Answer

5 significant figures. The leading zeros are not significant. The zero between 3 and 4 and the two trailing zeros to the right of the decimal are significant.

4 pts2

Write 0.00067200 m in scientific notation while preserving all significant figures.

Answer

6.7200 × 10⁻⁴ m.

4 pts3

A student multiplies 12.40 cm by 3.2 cm and reports 39.68 cm². What is wrong with the reported answer, and what should the final result be?

Answer

The multiplication is numerically correct, but 3.2 has only 2 significant figures. The final answer should be 40 cm² or 4.0 × 10¹ cm² to show 2 significant figures.

4 pts4

Two balances display the same object as 8.4 g and 8.400 g. Which balance is more precise, and what does the extra information tell you?

Answer

The balance reporting 8.400 g is more precise because it reports to the thousandths place and therefore communicates finer measurement resolution.

4 pts5

Round 98,650 to 3 significant figures and write the result in a form that makes the number of significant figures unambiguous.

Answer

9.87 × 10⁴.

Part B · Dimensional Analysis, Density & Experimental Reasoning

32 points

8 pts6

A delivery truck travels 125 miles in 2.50 hours. Convert its average speed to meters per second using 1 mile = 1.609 km. Show one continuous dimensional-analysis chain.

Answer

125 mi/2.50 h = 50.0 mi/h. 50.0 mi/h × 1.609 km/mi × 1000 m/km × 1 h/3600 s = 22.3 m/s.

8 pts7

A metal sphere has a mass of 532 g and volume 59.4 cm³. Calculate its density. A reference table lists iron at 7.87 g/cm³, nickel at 8.90 g/cm³, and copper at 8.96 g/cm³. Which material is the sphere most consistent with?

Answer

Density = 532/59.4 = 8.96 g/cm³, matching copper most closely.

8 pts8

A student measures the volume of an irregular rock by water displacement but does not fully submerge the rock. Predict whether the calculated density will be too high or too low and explain.

Answer

The displaced volume will be too small because part of the rock remains above water. Since density = mass/volume, using a volume that is too small makes the calculated density too high.

8 pts9

A block has mass 4.80 kg and density 6.00 g/cm³. Determine its volume in liters. Then explain whether it would fit completely inside a 750 mL container.

Answer

4.80 kg = 4800 g. Volume = 4800/6.00 = 800 cm³ = 0.800 L. It would not fit in a 750 mL container because 800 mL > 750 mL.

Part C · Isotopes, Radiation & Chemical Reasoning

28 points

7 pts10

An element has isotopes of 10.01 amu at 19.9% abundance and 11.01 amu at 80.1% abundance. Calculate its average atomic mass.

Answer

(10.01)(0.199)+(11.01)(0.801)=10.81 amu approximately.

7 pts11

An element has two isotopes: 62.93 amu and 64.93 amu. Its average atomic mass is 63.55 amu. Determine the abundance of the heavier isotope.

Answer

Let x be the heavier fraction: 62.93(1−x)+64.93x=63.55. x=0.31, so the heavier isotope is about 31%.

7 pts12

Wave A has wavelength 7.50 × 10⁻⁷ m. Calculate its frequency. Then state whether Wave A has more or less photon energy than a wave with wavelength 2.50 × 10⁻⁷ m.

Answer

ν=(3.00×10⁸)/(7.50×10⁻⁷)=4.00×10¹⁴ Hz. The 2.50×10⁻⁷ m wave has shorter wavelength and therefore higher frequency and greater photon energy, so Wave A has less energy.

7 pts13

A white powder dissolves in water. When the water evaporates, the same white solid remains. Later, a separate sample of the powder is strongly heated and produces a new gas and a different-colored solid. Which observations indicate physical change and which indicate chemical change?

Answer

Dissolving and recovering the same solid by evaporation are physical changes. Producing a gas and a different-colored solid upon heating indicates chemical change because new substances formed.

Part D · Distinct Honors Synthesis

20 points

10 pts14

You are given a mixture of iron filings, sand, salt, and water. Design a complete separation procedure that recovers all four components as well as practical. For each step, identify the physical property that makes the separation possible.

Answer

Use a magnet to remove iron filings by magnetism. Filter or sieve to separate sand because it is insoluble and physically larger. Distill or evaporate the water to separate it from dissolved salt based on volatility/boiling point, leaving salt behind.

10 pts15

A laboratory obtains three density measurements for the same metal: 7.82, 7.79, and 9.21 g/cm³. Explain what should be done before calculating an average. Then propose one plausible experimental cause for the inconsistent trial.

Answer

The 9.21 g/cm³ value should be investigated as a possible outlier rather than automatically averaged. The measurement should be checked or repeated. Possible causes include an incorrect volume reading, trapped air, incomplete submersion, transcription error, or a balance error.

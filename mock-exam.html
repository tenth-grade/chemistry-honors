Part A · Measurement & Significant Figures

20 points

4 pts1

Report 0.0060780 kg to 4, 3, 2, and 1 significant figures.

Answer

4 sf: 0.006078 kg; 3 sf: 0.00608 kg; 2 sf: 0.0061 kg; 1 sf: 0.006 kg.

4 pts2

Write 58,900,000 m in scientific notation with exactly 3 significant figures.

Answer

5.89 × 10⁷ m.

4 pts3

Calculate (12.44 × 3.2) ÷ 0.875 and report the final result with the correct number of significant figures.

Answer

Calculator value ≈45.4949. The limiting value has 2 significant figures, so the answer is 45.

4 pts4

Evaluate 18.347 mL + 2.1 mL + 0.056 mL using the correct addition/subtraction precision rule.

Answer

20.503 mL before rounding. The least precise term is to the tenths place, so 20.5 mL.

4 pts5

Explain why 4,000 can be ambiguous with respect to significant figures and give three unambiguous scientific-notation forms representing 1, 2, and 4 significant figures.

Answer

Without a decimal point or context, trailing zeros may be placeholders. Examples: 4 × 10³, 4.0 × 10³, and 4.000 × 10³.

Part B · Dimensional Analysis & Density

28 points

7 pts6

A probe travels 7.25 × 10⁵ km in 6.40 days. Determine its average speed in m/s using one continuous dimensional-analysis chain.

Answer

7.25×10⁵ km ÷ 6.40 d ×1000 m/km ×1 d/24 h ×1 h/3600 s = 1.31×10³ m/s.

7 pts7

A rectangular metal block measures 5.20 cm × 2.40 cm × 18.0 mm and has mass 0.724 kg. Determine its density in g/cm³.

Answer

18.0 mm=1.80 cm. Volume=5.20×2.40×1.80=22.464 cm³. Mass=724 g. Density=32.229... g/cm³ → 32.2 g/cm³.

7 pts8

An irregular metal sample raises water in a graduated cylinder from 0.215 L to 0.468 L. If the metal has density 8.96 g/cm³, determine its mass in kilograms.

Answer

Displaced volume=0.253 L=253 cm³. Mass=8.96×253=2266.88 g=2.27 kg.

7 pts9

A block has mass 6.540 kg and density 11.3 g/cm³. It is submerged in a tank initially containing 1.250 L of water. What final volume should the tank read?

Answer

6540 g ÷11.3 g/cm³=578.76 cm³=0.579 L. Final=1.250+0.579=1.829 L.

Part C · Isotopes & Electromagnetic Radiation

32 points

8 pts10

An element has isotopes of 63.929 amu at 69.2% abundance and 65.926 amu at 30.8% abundance. Calculate the average atomic mass.

Answer

(63.929)(0.692)+(65.926)(0.308)=64.54 amu approximately.

8 pts11

An element has two isotopes: 78.918 amu and 80.916 amu. Its average atomic mass is 79.904 amu. Determine the percent abundance of the heavier isotope.

Answer

Let x be the heavier fraction: 78.918(1−x)+80.916x=79.904. x≈0.4935, so 49.35%.

8 pts12

Three isotopes have masses 27.977 amu at 92.23%, 28.976 amu at 4.67%, and an unknown mass at 3.10%. The average atomic mass is 28.086 amu. Determine the unknown isotope mass.

Answer

28.086=(27.977)(0.9223)+(28.976)(0.0467)+m(0.0310). Solving gives approximately 29.98 amu.

8 pts13

Electromagnetic radiation has wavelength 4.25 × 10⁻⁷ m. Calculate its frequency. A second wave has twice this wavelength. Without recalculating from scratch, state the second wave's frequency.

Answer

ν=(3.00×10⁸)/(4.25×10⁻⁷)=7.06×10¹⁴ Hz. Doubling wavelength halves frequency, so the second wave is 3.53×10¹⁴ Hz.

Part D · Honors Synthesis

20 points

10 pts14

A fictional mineral has density 6.85 g/cm³ and sells for $145 per 12.0 cm³. A manufacturer needs 425 kg of it for one machine. With a budget of $850,000, determine how many complete machines can be supplied and how much budget remains. Show the dimensional path clearly.

Answer

425 kg=425,000 g. Volume=425,000/6.85≈62,044 cm³. Cost per machine≈62,044/12.0×$145≈$749,700. Therefore 1 complete machine can be supplied, leaving approximately $100,300. Differences of a few hundred dollars can occur depending on guard digits.

10 pts15

A spacecraft travels to a destination 4.10 light years away. Use 1 light year = 5.879 × 10¹² miles, 1 mile = 1.609 km, and spacecraft speed 2.95 × 10⁸ m/s. Determine the travel time in years using 365.25 days/year. Then explain why your result is close to, but not exactly, 4.10 years.

Answer

Convert light years→miles→km→m, divide by m/s, then convert seconds→days→years. The result is about 4.17 years. It is slightly greater than 4.10 years because the craft travels below the speed of light.
